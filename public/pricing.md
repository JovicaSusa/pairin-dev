# Pricing — Pairin

## Free (Beta)
- Price: $0/month
- Limits: Unlimited pair programming sessions
- Features: Live matching, scheduled pair requests, session retros, community access, match by tech stack and experience level
- Availability: Free for all users during the beta period

Pairin is currently in public beta. All features are free while in beta. Paid tiers have not been announced yet.
